# Treehole Archive

**A place for stories that might otherwise go unrecorded.**

[Visit Treehole Archive](https://treeholearchive.com/) · [Try Past or Prompt](https://treeholearchive.com/game)

## About

Treehole Archive is a web platform for collecting, publishing, and preserving first-person life stories. People can contribute accounts of their experiences and organize them into collections, making personal histories easier to find and revisit.

I built Treehole to extend my work on the **Silicon Valley History Archive** beyond the people I could personally interview. An interview archive depends on someone going out and asking questions; Treehole is an attempt to give more people a place to record their own stories.

The larger question behind the project is simple: **whose experiences enter the historical record, and whose are left out?**

## Features

- **First-person publishing:** A place for contributors to write and preserve their own accounts.
- **User-created collections:** Organize stories around people, communities, or themes.
- **Searchable archive:** Browse stories and collections, including material from the Silicon Valley History Archive.
- **Public website:** Explore the project at [treeholearchive.com](https://treeholearchive.com/).
- **Past or Prompt:** An associated educational game that challenges players to distinguish authentic historical sources from AI-generated fabrications. [Play here](https://treeholearchive.com/game).

## Origins and related projects

Treehole grew out of the [Silicon Valley History Archive](https://peilinw2008.wixsite.com/history-archive), a collection of oral histories of people whose contributions to Silicon Valley are often overlooked. That work also informed *The Hidden Valley*, a self-published collection of 15 oral histories.

Where the original archive began with face-to-face interviews, Treehole explores how a digital platform can make personal history more participatory and persistent.

## Privacy and responsible archiving

Personal histories may include sensitive details about individuals and communities. Contributors' consent, appropriate visibility settings, and careful handling of personal data are important to the purpose of the project. Please do not copy, redistribute, or expose submitted personal stories without appropriate permission.
